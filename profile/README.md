# SumaqAgro

**SumaqAgro** is an **open-source precision agriculture platform** developed by **Dymbia**, a technology startup focused on building innovative digital solutions for the agricultural sector.

The platform is designed for small and medium-sized farmers, agricultural cooperatives, and technical advisors in Peru, with a particular emphasis on Andean potato cultivation and specialty coffee production.

SumaqAgro integrates open satellite imagery, financial management, and digital quality certification into a unified platform, enabling data-driven agricultural decision-making without requiring physical ground sensors.

## Core Features

- **Satellite Health & Moisture Monitoring:** Visualization of vegetation (NDVI) and water (NDWI) indices using satellite imagery to monitor crop conditions, detect agricultural stress, and support preventive decision-making.
- **Lot-Level Cost Accounting Engine:** Management of operational expenses at the agricultural lot level, including automated financial break-even calculations and production cost tracking.
- **Digital Harvest Quality Scoring & Certification:** Generation of digital quality certificates for potato grading and specialty coffee cup profiles, supported by QR codes for verification and traceability.
